# Abstract

This project takes the information visualization design of “Splendid China” as its research object. It focuses on five aspects of Chinese national culture: geography, language, food, architecture, and musical instruments. The project explores how multi-source cultural materials can be organized and transformed into graphic expressions in visual communication design. It aims to respond to problems such as scattered cultural information, diverse categories, and relatively high reading barriers. Through data collection, data cleaning, information classification, chart construction, and visual translation, the project forms a series of infographics with visual consistency and recognizability.

The first chapter discusses the policy background, theoretical value, practical significance, and research status at home and abroad, so as to clarify the basis and necessity of the topic. The second chapter defines the overall plan, design framework, case references, and design positioning. The third chapter explains the construction of the narrative framework, data collection, data cleaning, and dataset production. The fourth chapter describes the design process of typography, color, logo system, layout, renderings, and optimization. The fifth chapter presents the extended design, including webpage interaction, exhibition boards, and physical display. Finally, five series-based infographics and related extended applications are completed.

The study shows that information visualization can improve the organization efficiency and visual readability of ethnic cultural materials. It also helps audiences understand complex cultural content in a more intuitive and continuous way. This project provides a practical reference for undergraduate design projects related to cultural information visualization.

**Keywords:** Splendid China; Ethnic Culture; Information Visualization; Visual Communication Design
